20MnCr5 (1.7147) Alloy Steel

Case-Hardening Steel for High-Wear, High-Stress Components

Grade Description: 20MnCr5 is a chromium-manganese case-hardening alloy steel designed to combine a hard, wear-resistant surface with a tough core. Its heat-treatment response makes it widely used for gears, shafts and other highly stressed components.

Heat Treatment Guide

Predictable response across forging, carburizing, hardening and tempering processes.

  1. 200°C400°C600°C800°C1000°C
  2. Hot Forging1100–850°C
  3. Normalizing850–880°C
  4. Soft Annealing650–700°C
  5. Carburizing900–950°C
  6. Core Hardening850–880°C
  7. Case Hardening810–840°C
  8. Tempering150–200°C
