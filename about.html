Our Story

Blue Ocean Steels LLP was founded in December 2018 with a clear purpose: to bring structure, transparency and reliability to special steel supply chains.

The company was built to solve a real gap in the industry. Steel mills work with bulk heat loads and planned production cycles, while component manufacturers often need smaller, precise quantities delivered on time. BOS was created to bridge this gap with a system-driven supply model, strong industry knowledge and dependable execution.

Today, BOS is not just a steel supplier or stockist. It is a growth partner for manufacturers, helping them source the right quality steel, in the right quantity, with better visibility and confidence.

Our Team

The people behind BOS's systems, execution and customer trust

Arvind Sangamnerkar

Head of Sales and Marketing

Arvind Sangamnerkar is currently the Head of Marketing at Blue Ocean Steels, with over 5 years of experience in this role. He has a robust career spanning over 30 years in steel marketing, having worked at companies like Sunflag, MUSCO, Welspun, and Kalyani Steels.

At BOS, he is focused on marketing, supply chain management, ERP systems, and logistics. His efforts have helped streamline operations and enhance the company's reputation in the steel sector. His leadership in the marketing department has been instrumental in developing the company's strategic direction, ensuring it continues to meet the evolving demands of the steel industry.

Ganesh Malave

Head-Sourcing and Logistics

Ganesh Malave is the Head of Purchase and Logistics at Blue Ocean Steels with over 29 years of experience in the steel industry, specializing in specialty carbon and alloy steel long products. He is responsible for driving business growth, managing supply chain operations, and building strong client relationships.

Prior to this, Ganesh had experience as Manager Logistics and Marketing at Kalyani Steels for 19 years. His focus is on optimizing supply chains, ensuring timely deliveries, and aligning products with clients' needs.

Vilas Patil

Head- International Business & Institutional Sales

Vilas Patil is the Head of International Business & Corporate Accounts at Blue Ocean Steels, where he has been leading efforts to expand global markets for specialty carbon and alloy steel long products for over 3 years. Prior to this, he was a Team Leader at companies like Ispat profile and Kalyani Steels overseeing the supply of materials to Aluminum smelting industries, forging and seamless tube manufacturing.

Vilas gained significant experience of 30+ years in Domestic and international marketing. With his expertise in business strategy, negotiation, and collaboration, Vilas has contributed to Blue Ocean Steels' growth in both domestic and international market segments.

CSR Activities

Supporting society through meaningful action

Management and the team at Blue Ocean Steels believe that organisational growth is truly meaningful only when it also supports society.
