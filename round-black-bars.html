Round Black Bars

Prime round black steel bars for precision manufacturing

  1. Home
  2. Services
  3. Hot Rolled & Forged Longs
  4. Round Black Bars

Round black bars are solid, circular steel bars used as a fundamental raw material in precision manufacturing and heavy drop forging. They are produced by rolling or forging heated steel into uniform round sections, giving them the perfect balance of strength, ductility, machinability, and cost efficiency for further processing.

At Blue Ocean Steels (BOS), we supply only prime-quality material sourced to match your exact grade, size, chemistry, and supply condition. We connect tier-1 mill capabilities with your production needs, ensuring dependable steel supply for critical downstream applications across the automotive, defense, aerospace, and heavy engineering sectors.

Technical Specifications & Supply Conditions

Standard Size Ranges Available

  • Hot Rolled Round Bars: 16mm to 200mm diameter (Ready Stock & MTO)
  • Forged Round Bars: 160mm to 800mm diameter (Make-to-Order)

Custom cut-to-size bandsaw cutting is available across all diameters.

Supply Condition Best Suited For Technical Benefit
As Rolled Standard hot forging, basic machining Cost-effective baseline; grain structure is dependent on the mill's finishing temperature.
As Forged Heavy equipment, large-scale open die forging Ensures complete consolidation of the core for massive cross-sections.
Normalized Components requiring uniform strength Refines the grain structure to be uniform and predictable after hot working.
Annealed Heavy machining operations Softens the steel and relieves internal stresses, maximizing CNC insert tool life.
Hardened & Tempered (H&T) Direct-to-finish manufacturing Supplied at the required mechanical strength, eliminating the need for in-house heat treatment.
Spheroidised Annealed Cold heading, severe cold forming Alters carbides into a spherical shape, providing the maximum possible ductility for cold working.

Manufacturing Process at Quality Mills

1

EAF/BOF melting with LF refining and Vacuum Degassing (VD) for controlled chemistry and ultra-clean steel.

2

Continuous casting into billets/blooms with strictly controlled cooling to prevent center segregation.

3

Hot rolling or heavy open-die forging into uniform round bar sections with strict diameter control.

4

UT, MPI, Eddy Current, dimensional, and surface quality checks.
