Jominy Hardenability Calculator

Estimate the hardenability of alloy steel based on its chemical composition and distance from the quenched end. The Jominy hardenability calculator helps metallurgists and engineers understand the expected hardness response of steel during heat treatment, supporting steel grade selection and component manufacturing decisions.

When to Use

  • To estimate the hardenability of alloy steel grades.
  • To compare expected hardness values at different Jominy distances.
  • To support steel selection and heat treatment planning for critical engineering components.
