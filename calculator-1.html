Reduction Ratio Calculator

Calculate the reduction ratio of steel billets, blooms and bars during forging or rolling operations. The steel reduction ratio calculator compares the initial and final cross-sectional areas to help engineers understand the extent of material deformation during processing.

When to Use

  • To calculate reduction ratios for steel forging and rolling operations.
  • To evaluate dimensional changes from billets or blooms to finished steel bars.
  • To support manufacturing planning and material quality requirements.
