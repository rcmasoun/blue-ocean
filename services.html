Contract Manufacturing

From Raw Steel to Ready-to-Use Components

Blue Ocean Steels (BOS) provides end-to-end steel contract manufacturing solutions, connecting OEMs and industrial buyers with a reliable network of manufacturing partners across India.

From raw material sourcing and value-added steel bars to forged blanks and fully CNC-machined components, we simplify the manufacturing process through a single, dependable supply chain partner.
