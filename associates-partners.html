01

Jindal Steel Limited have appointed Blue Ocean Steels as their exclusive Marketing Partner for Alloy Steel Products in Maharashtra

JSPL is a leading steel manufacturer in India, with presence in steel, power, mining, oil & gas and infrastructure. With a turnover of approx USD 3.3 billion, JSPL is a part of USD 18 billion diversified Jindal Group Conglomerate.

BOS is now exclusive marketing partner for Carbon and Alloy steel bars, billets, blooms and wire rods manufactured by JSPL, catering to customers in Maharashtra across industry segments like automotive, bearings, oil & gas, fasteners, material handling, general engineering and several others.

02

ABS Steel, Italy have appointed Blue Ocean Steels as the official Channel Partner for their Alloy Steel Products in India

ABS was established in 1988, after the merging of two highly qualified and long-experienced companies, ‘Officine Bertoli’ founded in 1813 and ‘Safau’, whose origin dates back to 1934. ABS is now part of Danieli Group.

They specialize in large blooms (upto 850mm dia), large ingots (upto 22T square, upto 28T round), rolled bars (up to 500mm) and forged bars (upto 1030mm square, upto 800mm square).

BOS will assist ABS in establishing a customer base in India, and will also handle the import, warehousing, sales & distribution of steel manufactured by ABS.

03

Blue Ocean Steels have now joined hands with GULF MARKETS INTERNATIONAL for supply of various industrial products to the Middle East

Gulf Markets International is active in the field of industrial supplies and services for more than 40 years. GMI supplies to various customers in the GCC region on spot and long terms contractual basis, for different industrial sectors such as Aluminum, Oil & Gas, Cement, Iron & Steel, Glass and others.

Thanks to its strong and extended ties with Tier One suppliers and principals, GMI succeeded in building a versatile sourcing platform over the years with more than 40 companies currently in its portfolio from different parts of the world, including Europe, Canada, USA, India, China & the GCC.

04

Sanyo Special Steel Manufacturing India Pvt. Ltd. (formerly MUSCO) is one of the major sources for Die Steel through our partner company GA Steels

Sanyo Special Steel Manufacturing India Pvt. Ltd. is a leading, pioneer manufacturer of Carbon, Alloy & Tool Die Steels in As Cast, Forged, Rolled, Turned, Heat treated and Ring forged supply condition.

The long products are with different shapes of Ingots, Blooms, Slabs Billets, Bars, Rings and are offered in various sizes as per national, international and Customers’ specifications. These high quality steels find applications in different sectors of industries like Automobile, Bearing, Engineering, Oil gas & Mining, Railways etc.

06

Shahnaz Bright Steel Industries Pvt Ltd

Shahnaz Bright Steel Industries Pvt Ltd is an ISO 9001:2015 Certified in Bright Steel Manufacturer, Supplier and Dealers of finished products in Domestic and the International market. The company has the most elaborate and sophisticated infrastructure to produce cold drawn, centreless turned and centreless ground bars.

The company plant is equipped with a range of machines and advanced processing facilities that give flexibility to meet the needs of customers. Importantly, we commit to satisfy customers by supplying consistent quality of bright steels, drawn and ground Enla products to domestic and international markets as per customer requirements.

SPINDEL-UND LAGERUNGSTECHNIK FRAUREUTH GMBH

Blue Ocean Steels (BOS) is associated with SLF GmbH and its sister company, STI Industrial. SLF GmbH, headquartered in Fraureuth, Germany, is a renowned manufacturer of high-speed spindles and precision bearings, while STI Industrial is recognized as a reliable supplier of industrial bearings. This strategic association enables BOS to offer world-class, precision-engineered solutions to our customers, ensuring enhanced performance and quality across diverse industrial applications.

Product Specialty for Bearings

  • High-precision Spindle bearings (DGBB, ACBB, CRB)
  • Current-insulating coated bearings
  • Hybrid bearings (Ceramic Balls and Rollers)
  • Corrosion and wear resistant bearings
  • Bearing components made of special materials
  • Bearings with special seals and special greasing
  • Thin section bearings

Product Specialty in Spindle Solution for each application

  • Workpiece spindles / tailstock sleeves
  • Drilling and milling spindles
  • Dressing spindles
  • Balancing and test spindles

  • Spindle components

Product Specialty for Bearings (STI)

ONLYGOOD
